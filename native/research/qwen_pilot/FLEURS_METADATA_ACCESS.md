## Follow-up grouping and first operational media check

An independent connected-component audit joined recordings, source documents, sentence IDs and normalized English prompts before selecting an operational reserve. The 40 published human-baseline examples expand to 40 document components, 48 recordings and 177 annotation rows. All five publisher signer groups occur in those 40 examples. Training on other FLEURS recordings therefore does not automatically leave that whole assessment signer-independent.

The 20 distinct source paths in the earlier Meta pilot join 14 FLEURS document components (17 recordings, 69 rows). Three of those components also belong to the reserved assessment. None of the 40 individual human sentence references exactly matches a prior normalized pilot prompt. This is known document-family exposure, not proof of direct target leakage or that every baseline example is contaminated. The union reserve covers 51 document components, 62 recordings and 236 rows. Unmatched prior prompts, broader cross-source document links, near duplicates and base-model pretraining exposure remain unresolved.

Metadata also contains 25 recordings with an overlap between adjacent sentence windows, up to 2.28 seconds, plus 1,219 positive gaps and ten exactly abutting boundaries. These may reflect translation/coarticulation or annotation alignment; they are not automatically invalid. Do not assume sentence cuts contain only the intended utterance or silently trim overlaps to force disjointness. The zero-shot setting has validation/test roles and no training role; the other settings supply training assignments.
